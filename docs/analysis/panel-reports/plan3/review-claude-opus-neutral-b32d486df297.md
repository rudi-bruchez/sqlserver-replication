## Concluded by reading

1. The cost of `add_article` contradicts its tests, and under-reports. Task 7 classes `add_article` as `snapshot`; Task 9 says "`snapshot` gives R11's", and R11 gives "full generation deferred" when ready and kept `immediate_sync`, "online otherwise", on the ground that a snapshot-class change "marks nothing". `sp_addarticle` marks the new article (`15-snapshot-scope C.native.addarticle`: `m_t4=1`, `awaits=yes`). `TestAddArticleImmediateSync` expects `[new_snapshot]` (the rules give `full_generation_deferred`) and `TestLabAddArticle` expects `new_snapshot` on a publication without `immediate_sync` (the rules give `online`). Either the tests fail or the implementer invents a special case; implemented by the rules, adding an article to `SalesRef` prints `online` for an operation that needs a snapshot run (anti-goal 1).

2. Hand-written files have properties with no measured default, which rule 5 refuses. Rule 5 refuses "a nil property with no measured default" and rule 6 covers only the distributor, `identity_range_management`, schedules and the snapshot agent's `job_enabled`. Nothing covers an agent's `profile`, `job_name` (`11-defaults` rows are `REPLLAB-m_pubdb-<id>`, not expressible), `run_as`, `command`, the `job_enabled` of a push or log reader agent, or `publisher.sync_with_backup` (the only `D.sp_replicationdboption.publish` rows are two `no-column` flags). `notCompared` is a diff rule, not a normalization rule. The files of Tasks 19 and 20 are described without any of these, so they are refused before anything runs, unless the implementer writes every agent property into them; the plan says neither. Related: rule 3 qualifies only command procedures; a `filter.procedure`, `sync_object` or `source` written without `schema` stays `""` against the exported `dbo` (an `sp_articlefilter` accepts `[dbo].[x]` and `dbo.x` and stores `dbo`, measured), so "procedure `m_flt1`" in `create.yaml` replans a reinitializing filter change forever, and an unqualified `source` replans the R20 drop and add of the article on every plan.
